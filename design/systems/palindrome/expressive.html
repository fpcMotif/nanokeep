Palin­drome

Palin­drome

One idea runs through every screen. Each measurement has a pair, and the pair meets at a spine. Upload faces download. CPU faces memory. Space you take back faces space you keep.

The system covers the three jobs of nanokeep: watching processes, watching the network, and cleaning disk space. Every screen below is one window at 1240 × 780.

Colour

Three signal colours, each with one job. Violet is the spine and the CPU. Sand is memory and download. Lime is upload and reclaimed space.

Type

Syne carries headlines and hero numbers. Hanken Grotesk carries reading text. Every number on a data surface is JetBrains Mono with tabular figures.

Rules

Four rules that every screen follows.

The spine

Paired values grow away from one shared axis. You compare both sides at a glance and never read a legend.

Mirrored corners

Cards on the left round their outer corners 9 px and their inner corners 2 px. Cards on the right do the reverse.

The 7 · 1 · 7 grid

Paired views split into seven parts, one part, and seven parts. The narrow centre column holds the axis and its labels.

Nine

Spacing steps are 3, 6, 9, 18, 27 and 45 px. Padding inside a window is 18 px top and 27 px at the sides.
